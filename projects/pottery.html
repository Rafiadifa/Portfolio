The Concept

Beautiful and unhealing

“Our love is like a bruise, I hope it won't heal, 'cause it's you. You'll always be my Blue Valentine.”

NMIXX · “Blue Valentine”

Just as a bruise is a mark left by a touch that stayed too long, this rose holds the pieces of you that are too delicate to carry alone.

It's a Blue Valentine for the love that hurts just enough to feel real, a place to rest your most precious things where they can remain, like a bruise, beautiful and unhealing.
