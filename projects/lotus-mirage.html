The Lotus Mirage

A virtual-reality lotus garden, and the pavilion at its heart — a beautiful shell that turns out to be hollow. A space about the distance between how grand something looks from the outside and how empty it can feel within.

The Concept

A beautiful lie

The grandness of the exterior sets up the emptiness within.

The interior is blindingly bright and disturbingly empty. Because the walls are glass and there is a massive hole — an Oculus — in the ceiling, there are no shadows to hide in. The grandness of the exterior was a lie; inside, there is no furniture, no warmth, just a hollow, echoing chamber of glass and steel.

Those inside desperately stretch toward the hole in the roof, reaching for the sky they can see but never touch.
