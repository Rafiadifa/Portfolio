The Lotus Mirage

A virtual-reality lotus garden built to carry a short story — a quiet, walkable space you can step into and explore. I modelled the lotus itself; the surrounding garden was a team build.

The Concept

A garden you can walk through

The full concept write-up lives in the University Portfolio — linked below.

The Lotus Mirage turns a short story into a place. Rather than telling it on a page, the narrative unfolds as you move through a lotus garden in VR — the lotus at its centre modelled by hand, set within a scene the team built around it.
