04 / About

About me

Architecture student at Tsinghua. I design, make things, and keep a record of it here.

I'm Rafi (Rafi Adifa Maulana), an architecture student at Tsinghua University. This site is where I keep the work I care about in one place: spatial and architectural projects, some design and craft, and the drawings I do on the side.

I like jumping between very different scales, from a VR pavilion to a college mascot to a single ceramic flower. A lot of it is group work, and where it is, I credit the whole team.
