04 — About

About me

Architecture student at Tsinghua — designing, making, and documenting along the way.

I'm Rafi (Rafi Adifa Maulana) — an architecture student at Tsinghua University. This site is where I keep the work I care about in one place: architectural and spatial studies, design and craft projects, and the drawings in between.

I like work that moves between scales — from a VR pavilion to a college mascot to a single ceramic flower. Some projects here are group work; where that's the case, the whole team is credited.
